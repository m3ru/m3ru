Morning (prAta) Sandhyavandanam

The designers of this ritual captured the essence of taking down two birds with one stone, as practicing Sandhyavandanam not only spiritually enhances us but also provides really helpful discipline and structure to mornings. Admittedly, it would be best to practice trikAla (thrice per day). This post will delineate my personal morning routine, a specifically shortened version (laghu sandhyavandanama) that I carry with me everywhere.

Text may be warped or unreadable without noto-sans-cjk or equivalent devanagari fonts installed.

Justifications of Sandhyavandanam (not recited)

उ॒द्यन्त॑मस्तं॒यन्त॑मादि॒त्यम॑भिध्या॒यन्कु॒र्वन्ब्रा”ह्म्॒णो वि॒द्वान्थ्स॒कलं॑ भ॒द्रम॑श्नुते॒ऽसावा॑दि॒त्यो ब्र॒ह्मेति॒ ब्रह्मै॒व सन्ब्रह्मा॒प्येति॒ य ए॒वं वेद॑
A brAhmaNa who contemplates on the rising and setting of the sun and does obeisance with gAyatri attains all bliss because this Aditya is the Supreme Brahman.
सन्ध्याहीनोऽशुचिर्नित्यं अनर्हस्सर्व कर्मसु। यदन्यत् कुरुते कर्म न तस्य फलभाग्भवेत्॥
He who does not perform Sandhyavandanam every day is ever impure and, consequently, unfit to perform any vedic rites. Whatever other rites he may perform, he will not obtain the fruit thereof.
श्रुति: स्मृति: ममैवाज्ञा यस्यां उल्लङ्घ्य वर्तते। आज्ञाच्छेदी मम द्रोही मद्भक्तोपि न वैष्ण्व​:॥
The vedas and the treatises thereunder, that is, smritis, convey My commands. Whoever violates them, commits treason. Even if he be My devotee, he is by no means a vaishnava.

Step 1: Purification of the Mind and Body

अपवित्र​: पवित्रो वा सर्वावस्थां गतोऽपि वा। य​: स्मरेत् पुण्डरीकाक्षं स बाह्याभ्यन्तर​: शुचि:॥
This is chanted to acknowledge the lack of cleanliness the mind and body carry and to ensure that by remembering Vishnu we are clean.
